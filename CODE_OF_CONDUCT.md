# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in UsageMax Display welcoming and
respectful for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or
ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in discussion and review.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Accept differing opinions and corrections in good faith.
- Protect privacy: do not post credentials, customer data, private telemetry,
  or another person's identifying information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate
privacy violations, and knowingly submitting malicious or credential-bearing
material are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently
restrict participation for behavior that violates this policy. To report a
conduct concern, contact the project maintainers privately through the repository
hosting platform. Share only the context needed to investigate.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/.
